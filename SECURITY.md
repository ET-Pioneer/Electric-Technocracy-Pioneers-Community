# Security Policy: Safeguarding the Global Transition

The **Electric Technocracy Pioneers Community** recognizes that the security of our technological and legal frameworks is the cornerstone of planetary trust. In a system governed by **Artificial Superintelligence (ASI)** and **Direct Digital Democracy (DDD)**, protecting the "code of law" and the "code of machines" is essential to preventing systemic disruption. 

We maintain a proactive stance toward vulnerability management to ensure a stable, peaceful transition to a post-scarcity world.

## 2. Reporting a Vulnerability

In an **Electric Technocracy**, vulnerabilities are not just technical bugs; they are risks to the global social contract. We rely on our pioneers, researchers, and "white-hat" legal scholars to report flaws in our digital voting models, resource allocation algorithms, or treaty-chain documentation.



### How to Report
To protect the integrity of the **World Succession Deed** and the global governance structure, please **do not use public GitHub issues** for security reports. Use our coordinated disclosure process:

1.  **Encrypted Contact:** Send a report to our Security Task Force at **security@diplomats.com** or **singularity@diplomats.com**.
2.  **Scope of Interest:** We are particularly interested in:
    * Flaws in the **Succession Logic** of the WSD 1400/98 framework.
    * Exploits in the **Direct Digital Democracy** voting protocols.
    * Theoretical attacks on the **Tech-Tax/UBI** distribution algorithms.
3.  **Required Documentation:** * A clear description of the vulnerability.
    * The potential impact on global stability or human rights.
    * Detailed steps to reproduce the issue or a logical proof of the theoretical flaw.

### Our Commitment to You
* **Acknowledgment:** You will receive an initial response from a community coordinator within **48 hours**.
* **Regular Updates:** We provide status updates every **7 days** during the assessment and remediation process.
* **Resolution:** Once a vulnerability is verified, we work toward a systemic "Patch" or a "Global Reset" of the affected module.
* **Recognition:** Valid reports that significantly enhance the security of the Technocracy will be permanently archived in our **Contributor Hall of Fame** (unless anonymity is requested).

---

## 3. Disclosure Policy
We advocate for **Responsible Disclosure**. We ask that researchers provide the Pioneers Community with a reasonable timeframe to address and neutralize the vulnerability before any public information is released. This ensures that the transition to an **Electronic Paradise** remains uninterrupted by malicious actors or institutional sabotage.

---

## 4. Security Principles of the Technocracy
* **Resilience by Design:** All governance models must be resistant to centralized manipulation or "51% attacks."
* **Quantum Integrity:** We prioritize quantum-resistant encryption for all digital democracy and UBI ledger protocols.
* **Transparent Verification:** All security patches are open-source and verifiable via our global decentralized archives.
