Contributing to the Electric Technocracy Pioneers Community

Welcome to the vanguard of post-national governance.

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Electric Technocracy Pioneers Community. We are an open-access, interdisciplinary think tank dedicated to architecting the transition to a society governed by Direct Digital Democracy, Artificial Superintelligence (ASI), and Post-Scarcity Economics.

We are not just writing code; we are writing the operating system for the future of civilization. Whether you are a legal scholar, an AI engineer, an economist, a philosopher, or a visionary artist, your contribution is vital.

1. Our Mission

Our goal is to create a robust, scientifically grounded, and legally sound framework for the Electric Technocracy. We reject the stagnation of traditional politics and nation-states in favor of a unified, abundant world.

Motto: No borders. No masters. Just progress.

2. How You Can Contribute

We welcome contributions in various forms. You do not need to be a developer to help building the future.

🔬 Research & Theory

  • Legal Analysis: Research on the Juridical Singularity, state succession laws, and the validity of the World Succession Deed 1400/98.
  • Economic Modeling: Simulations regarding Universal Basic Income (UBI), machine taxation, and post-scarcity resource allocation.
  • Governance Models: Essays and frameworks for Direct Digital Democracy and ASI-human collaboration.

💻 Technology & Code

  • Simulation Scripts: Python/R scripts for economic modeling.
  • Voting Mechanisms: Concepts for quantum-encrypted, transparent voting systems.
  • Data Visualization: Graphs and charts illustrating the transition to Electric Technocracy.

📝 Documentation & Wiki

  • Wiki Entries: expanding the knowledge base on key terms (e.g., “Tech Tax,” “Longevity Escape Velocity”).
  • Translation: Translating key manifestos and working papers into other languages to ensure global accessibility.

3. Contribution Workflow

We utilize a standard GitHub workflow.

  1. Find or Create an Issue:
    • Check the Issue Tracker to see if your idea is already being discussed.
    • If you have a new idea, open an issue labeled proposal or discussion to get feedback from the community before you start working.
  2. Fork & Branch:
    • Fork the repository.
    • Create a new branch for your work: git checkout -b feature/my-contribution.
  3. Submit a Pull Request (PR):
    • Submit your PR to the main branch.
    • In the description, explain why this change is necessary and how it advances the Electric Technocracy framework.
    • Link to any relevant research papers or legal documents.

4. Style & Standards

  • Tone: Maintain a professional, scientific, and objective tone. While our goals are radical, our methodology must be rigorous.
  • Format:
    • Documentation: Markdown (.md) or LaTeX (.tex) for complex papers.
    • Code: Clean, commented, and following standard PEP8 (Python) or relevant style guides.
  • Citations: When making factual claims about law or technology, please provide references (DOI links, treaty articles, or reputable sources).

5. Legal & Licensing (Important!)

By contributing to this repository, you agree to the following:

To ensure that the foundations of the new world remain free and accessible to all humanity, this community operates under the CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication).

  • You waive all copyright and related rights to your contribution.
  • You acknowledge that your work can be used, modified, and distributed by anyone, for any purpose, without restriction.
  • We do not accept contributions with restrictive licenses (e.g., GPL, CC-BY-NC). Knowledge must be free.

6. Code of Conduct

We are a “Free-Thinking Playground,” but we require mutual respect.

  • Debate Ideas, Not People: We discuss concepts, not personal attributes.
  • Scientific Rigor: Arguments should be backed by logic, data, or legal precedent, not ideology.
  • Inclusivity: We are building a world for everyone. Discrimination or harassment will result in an immediate ban.

Ready to build the Electronic Paradise? Start by browsing our Issues or introducing yourself in the discussions.
