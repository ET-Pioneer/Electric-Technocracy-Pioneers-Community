    🏛️ About the Pioneers Community

    Electric Technocracy Pioneers is an open scientific research hub and free‑access repository for exploring Electric Technocracy, AI Governance, the Judicial Singularity, the Technological Singularity, Artificial Superintelligence (ASI), AGI, Direct Digital Democracy (DDD), Universal Basic Income, and a United World of Abundance beyond nation‑states.

    The platform hosts openly licensed documents, papers, wiki articles, encyclopedia entries, audio, video, images, and slides, supporting global collaboration on post‑scarcity models, future governance, and transparent digital systems.

    ⚡ Electric Technocracy - A Visionary AI supported Direct Digital Democracy

    Electric Technocracy (ET) is the governance form that aligns with current and future technological development. It replaces traditional party politics with efficient, data-driven administration managed by Artificial Superintelligence (ASI). * Direct Digital Democracy: The population decides directly on proposals via secure online voting. * Post-Scarcity: Automation and robotics generate unprecedented wealth, distributed through a Universal Basic Income (UBI). * Abolition of Nations: Moving toward a united, peaceful world without the friction of national borders or partisan conflicts.

    ⚖️ SINGULARITY IN NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL LAW

    The Judicial Singularity is the legal foundation of the Technological Singularity. It represents the singular act of international law that enables a global restart of the legal system. * World Succession Deed 1400/98: The irreversible legal reality that serves as the catalyst for global succession. * Treaty Chains: Utilizing international treaty frameworks (NATO SOFA, HNS) to transition from national sovereignty to a global techno-legal order.

    📚 International Treaty Law WIKI

    The International Treaty Law WIKI is the scholarly encyclopedia for the Juridical Singularity. It serves as a central knowledge base for legal experts and researchers to document the evolution of international law towards a post-state era. It provides deep insights into the mechanisms of state succession and multilateral treaty structures.

    🔬 Scientific Research & Community

    The community focuses on Legal Engineering, Future Governance, and Post-Scarcity Economics. We publish working papers and preprints on Zenodo and GitHub to ensure that the transition to an AI-driven society is transparent, legally sound, and beneficial for all humanity.
