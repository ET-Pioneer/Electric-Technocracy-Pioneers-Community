📑 Inhaltsverzeichnis

1. Executive Summary


The Electric Technocracy Pioneers Community (ETPC)
is a global, open-access research collective and interdisciplinary think
tank. It operates at the cutting edge of the Technological
Singularity, Juridical Singularity, and the
practical implementation of Electric Technocracy.


As a collaborative nexus for scholars, engineers, and legal
theorists, the ETPC develops the structural blueprints for a
civilization that transcends traditional governance. By synthesizing
advanced AI theory with new-age international law, the community
provides a robust library of preprints, working papers, and technical
frameworks designed to navigate the transition into a post-scarcity
era.



2. Core Philosophy and
Identity


The ETPC is defined by a radical commitment to logic-driven societal
evolution. Its identity rests on three foundational imperatives:



  • The “Free-Thinking Playground”: A sanctuary for
    unconstrained intellectual exploration where the future is modeled
    through the lenses of radical equality and technical efficiency.

  • The Dissolution of the Westphalian Order: The
    community advocates for the systematic phasing out of nation-states and
    traditional political hierarchies. It seeks to end the era of
    geopolitical conflict and the systemic privileges of political
    elites.

  • Universal Human Integration: A vision of a unified
    global society where human rights are not merely legal suggestions but
    absolute, indivisible, and digitally enforced realities for every
    individual on Earth.



3. Foundational Theoretical
Frameworks


Electric Technocracy
(The Goeritz Model)


Originally conceptualized by R. Goeritz (2024), this
model serves as the community’s intellectual cornerstone. It shifts the
focus of governance from human fallibility to systemic intelligence. In
this model, society is organized around technological optimization,
ethical algorithms, and the total elimination of structural
scarcity.


The Juridical Singularity


This concept describes the inevitable convergence of fragmented
national legal systems into a single, unified global legal authority. *
The Shift: Moving from “horizontal” international law
(treaties between states) to a “vertical” global framework. *
The Result: A consolidated legal personality for all of
humanity, ending sovereign plurality in favor of a singular, transparent
jurisdiction.


Legal Anchor: WSD 1400/98


The World Succession Deed 1400/98 is the primary
legal instrument and historical blueprint utilized by the community. It
functions as the foundational document that establishes the legal path
toward global succession, providing the necessary continuity for a new
form of civilization to emerge within a recognized legal context.



4. The Five Pillars of
Progress


The ETPC research focuses on five critical sectors that define the
functional reality of an Electric Technocracy:



  1. Direct Digital Democracy (DDD): Utilizing
    quantum-encrypted communication networks to facilitate real-time, global
    decision-making. Every individual participates directly in the
    legislative process, removing the need for intermediary politicians or
    representatives.


  2. AI Governance & Artificial Superintelligence
    (ASI):     ASI acts as the neutral, data-driven administrative
    layer of society. It processes complex global variables to provide
    optimized policy recommendations focused exclusively on long-term human
    welfare and ecological balance.


  3. Post-Scarcity & Automated Robotics: The
    total replacement of human manual and cognitive labor with autonomous
    robotic systems. This pillar also investigates the “Ethics of the
    Machine,” exploring the rights of sentient AI and the integration of
    machines into the social fabric.


  4. The Tech-Tax & Universal Basic Income (UBI):
    Under this system, human beings are 100% tax-exempt. The state is funded
    through a “Tech Tax”—a levy on the productivity of
    automated systems and AI labor. This wealth is redistributed as a
    high-tier UBI, ensuring unconditional financial sovereignty for every
    person.


  5. Biotechnological Longevity: The application of
    AI-driven medical research and biotechnology to arrest and reverse
    biological aging. The goal is to extend the healthy human lifespan
    indefinitely, making death an elective rather than an inevitable
    event.




5. The
Laboratory of Civilization: Community Purpose


The ETPC functions as a “Living Laboratory.” It provides a platform
for: * Collaborative Peer Review: Scholars refine
models for machine-led resource distribution. * Economic
Simulation: Testing post-monetary and UBI-driven market
behaviors. * Open Access Repository: Ensuring that all
research, from technical reports to philosophical treatises, is
available to the public without paywalls.



6. Digital
Infrastructure and Archive (GitHub)


The ETPC utilizes a comprehensive GitHub Repository
as its permanent archival hub. This ensures the transparency and
reproducibility of all research. The archive includes: *
Technical Whitepapers: Detailed PDFs on AI governance
and legal singularity. * Multimedia Documentation:
Video podcasts, diagrams, and audio presentations. * Dynamic
Roadmaps: Strategic plans for the phase-by-phase implementation
of technocratic systems. * Raw Datasets: Information
used for modeling post-scarcity economies.



7. Intellectual
Ecosystem and Related Works


The community maintains deep links with a growing body of scientific
literature and philosophical works, most notably those authored by: *
R. Goeritz: The primary conceptual architect. *
Oliver Markus Reff & Yalcin Veddat Durkac: Key
contributors to the expansion of technocratic theory and its legal
implications.


Core Research Areas: * Evolutionary Governance *
Algorithmic Justice * Post-Work Sociology * Quantum Jurisprudence



8. Closing Mandate


The Electric Technocracy Pioneers Community is driven by a singular,
uncompromising vision of the future:



“No borders. No masters. Just progress.”



Through the marriage of intelligence and fairness, the ETPC is not
just predicting the future—it is engineering it.
