3. Core Conceptual Frameworks


I. Electric
Technocracy (The Governance Model)


Electric Technocracy is a proposed societal model where governance is
executed through Direct Digital Democracy (DDD)
supported by Artificial Superintelligence (ASI). *
No Politicians: The removal of intermediaries (parties,
parliaments) in favor of direct citizen participation via secure,
quantum-encrypted voting. * ASI as Administrator: AI
acts as a neutral facilitator, simulating policy outcomes and managing
logistics, while humans retain the final moral decision-making
power.


II. The
Juridical Singularity (The Legal Foundation)


Just as technology reaches a singularity, so too must the law. The
Juridical Singularity describes the collapse of
sovereign plurality (multiple nation-states) into a unified global legal
entity. * The World Succession Deed (WSD) 1400/98: This
document is researched here not just as a historical artifact, but as
the active legal instrument for global state succession, enabling the
peaceful dissolvement of borders. * Treaty Chains:
Analysis of how international law and treaties (NATO, UN, ITU) can be
legally transitioned into a singular global jurisdiction.


III.
Post-Scarcity Economics (The Economic Engine)


The Electric Technocracy operates on a model of absolute abundance
driven by automation. * The Machine Tax: Humans are
tax-exempt. Public revenue is generated exclusively by
taxing the productivity of robots, AI, and automated systems. *
Universal Basic Income (UBI): The wealth generated by
the “Machine Tax” is redistributed unconditionally to every human being,
decoupling survival from labor.
