What is Electric Technocracy?

Definition & Architecture

  • Post-national governance model: Replaces nation-state system with digitally coordinated, automation-supported planetary order

  • Direct Digital Democracy (DDD): Citizens propose, deliberate, and vote on legislation via cryptographically secured platforms

  • ASI as non-sovereign advisor: Artificial Superintelligence provides analysis, simulation, and optimization but holds no autonomous political authority

  • Humanity remains sovereign: All decision-making power resides with collective human consent (Encyclopedia.pub 59380)

Key Mechanisms & Goals

  • Tech Tax → UBI: Economy funded by machine taxation; Universal Basic Income as technological dividend; humans tax-free

  • Post-scarcity sustainability: Machine productivity, renewable energy, and circular economies eliminate structural scarcity

  • Transparency & ecology: Open-source algorithms, blockchain verification, and ecological integration as foundational principles

  • Ultimate objectives: Elimination of war, longevity for all, planetary administration based on equality and human flourishing

Electric Technocracy: A New Era of Human Flourishing

The Foundation: World Succession Deed 1400/98

The transition to a unified global society is not merely a political ambition but a legal reality established through the "World Succession Deed 1400/98". This document represents a "Juridical Singularity"—a unique act in international law that enables a global restart of the legal system. By utilizing a "Treaty Chain" (Vertragskette), it restructures the foundational agreements of the modern world, moving beyond the fragmented sovereignty of individual nation-states toward a cohesive, digitally coordinated planetary administration.

The Architecture of Direct Digital Democracy

Electric Technocracy replaces traditional representative politics with Direct Digital Democracy (DDD). In this model, citizens regain direct control over legislation, deliberating and voting via cryptographically secured platforms. Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) serves as a non-sovereign advisor, providing complex simulations and data-driven analysis to optimize societal outcomes while remaining strictly under human oversight. This ensures that while the administration is automated and efficient, the ultimate sovereignty always resides with collective human consent.

The Economic Engine: Machine Labor and UBI

The economic pillar of this new society is the "Technology Tax," which shifts the fiscal burden from human labor to machine productivity. As robots and AI handle the vast majority of industrial and administrative tasks, the resulting wealth is distributed back to all of humanity as an unconditional "technological dividend" or Universal Basic Income (UBI). This system effectively eliminates structural scarcity, allowing for a world without poverty or tax-driven inequality, and paving the way for "Longevity for All" and the pursuit of individual self-unfolding.
