What is Electric Technocracy?

Post-Scarcity Governance Model

Electric Technocracy is a comprehensive governance framework that envisions the transition from traditional nation-states to a unified, technologically-driven global system. Built on the foundation of the World Succession Deed (WSD) 1400/98, this model proposes a post-national structure where Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) serves as an advisory body for objective decision-making.

The system implements Direct Digital Democracy (DDD), enabling citizens to participate directly in governance through secure digital platforms. A technology tax on automation and AI-driven industries funds Universal Basic Income (UBI), ensuring economic security in a post-scarcity world where human labor becomes optional rather than mandatory.

The Juridical Singularity

From Territorial Sovereignty to Algorithmic Legitimacy

The Juridical Singularity represents the pivotal moment when legal systems transition from human-centric interpretation to AI-assisted or AI-driven jurisprudence. This transformation is grounded in international law principles, particularly through the Treaty Chain mechanism and the World Succession Deed 1400/98.

Völkerrechtliche Sukzession

The World Succession Deed (WSD) 1400/98 serves as the fundamental title for a global legal restart. By utilizing the "Treaty Chain" mechanism, it overrides obsolete nation-state structures, replacing them with a unified technical administration based on international law principles of state succession.

Electronic Paradise

The transition to an ASI-led advisory system ensures objective resource distribution via a technology tax. This funds the Universal Basic Income (UBI), effectively liberating humanity from the scarcity-based labor paradigm and enabling a society of abundance and creative freedom.

Electric Technocracy Pioneers Community

Open Scientific Research Hub

The Electric Technocracy Pioneers Community is an open-access repository and collaborative research platform dedicated to exploring the future of governance, law, and society. We host openly licensed documents, research papers, wiki articles, encyclopedia entries, and multimedia content supporting global collaboration on post-scarcity models, future governance systems, and transparent digital frameworks.

Our community brings together researchers, futurists, legal experts, technologists, and visionaries working toward a united world of abundance beyond traditional nation-states. All resources are freely accessible to support the advancement of knowledge and the transition to Electric Technocracy.

International Treaty Law WIKI

Comprehensive Legal Encyclopedia

The International Treaty Law WIKI serves as a comprehensive encyclopedia documenting international law, treaty systems, state succession principles, and the legal foundations for global governance transformation. This resource provides in-depth analysis of the Treaty Chain mechanism, the World Succession Deed 1400/98, and the juridical basis for post-national governance structures.

Accessible at wiki.free.nf, the WIKI offers open-access legal research supporting the theoretical and practical implementation of Electric Technocracy.
