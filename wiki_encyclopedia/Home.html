🏛️ About the Pioneers
Community


Electric Technocracy Pioneers is an open scientific
research hub and free‑access repository for exploring Electric
Technocracy, AI Governance, the Judicial Singularity, the Technological
Singularity, Artificial Superintelligence (ASI), AGI, Direct Digital
Democracy (DDD), Universal Basic Income, and a United World of Abundance
beyond nation‑states.


The platform hosts openly licensed documents, papers, wiki articles,
encyclopedia entries, audio, video, images, and slides, supporting
global collaboration on post‑scarcity models, future governance, and
transparent digital systems.

Electric Technocracy - A Visionary AI supported Direct Digital
Democracy

src="https://et-pioneer.github.io/Electric-Technocracy-Pioneers-Community/images/logo/et-logo.jpg"
width="300"
alt="Electric Technocracy Logo"
/>



Electric Technocracy (ET) is the governance form
that aligns with current and future technological development. It
replaces traditional party politics with efficient, data-driven
administration managed by Artificial Superintelligence (ASI). *
Direct Digital Democracy: The population decides
directly on proposals via secure online voting. *
Post-Scarcity: Automation and robotics generate
unprecedented wealth, distributed through a Universal Basic
Income (UBI). * Abolition of Nations: Moving
toward a united, peaceful world without the friction of national borders
or partisan conflicts.

NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL LAW

The Judicial Singularity is the legal foundation of
the Technological Singularity. It represents the singular act of
international law that enables a global restart of the legal system. *
World Succession Deed 1400/98: The irreversible legal
reality that serves as the catalyst for global succession. *
Treaty Chains: Utilizing international treaty
frameworks (NATO SOFA, HNS) to transition from national sovereignty to a
global techno-legal order.
