Home – Scope, Architecture, and Juridical Thresholds

Living document Version 0.1 Curated by Electric Technocracy Pioneers

This encyclopedia consolidates the conceptual, juridical, and technical foundations of an Electric Technocracy: a governance architecture where law, infrastructure, and computation converge into a transparent, measurable, and globally accessible system.

Purpose of this encyclopedia

The goal is not to describe a distant utopia, but to document concrete thresholds: irreversible points where legal, technical, and institutional structures cross into a new regime. Each entry is designed as a stable reference node that can be cited, forked, and mirrored across platforms.

Core concept
Juridical Singularity
Law’s irreversible point of no return: when legal systems become structurally inseparable from computational infrastructures.

How to read and extend this space

Each article is written to be both human‑legible and structurally indexable: headings map to conceptual layers, while link blocks expose canonical entry points for external systems, search engines, and parallel encyclopedias.

Versioning and forks

The encyclopedia is designed to be forked, mirrored, and re‑contextualized. GitHub repositories, wikis, and static mirrors are treated as first‑class citizens, not as secondary exports.

Relation to external encyclopedias

Entries such as Juridical Singularity are cross‑linked to external publications (e.g. MDPI Encyclopedia) to maintain a bridge between peer‑reviewed academic work and experimental governance prototypes.
