What is Electric Technocracy?

Definition & Architecture

  • Post-national governance model: Replaces nation-state system with digitally coordinated, automation-supported planetary order

  • Direct Digital Democracy (DDD): Citizens propose, deliberate, and vote on legislation via cryptographically secured platforms

  • ASI as non-sovereign advisor: Artificial Superintelligence provides analysis, simulation, and optimization but holds no autonomous political authority

  • Humanity remains sovereign: All decision-making power resides with collective human consent (Encyclopedia.pub 59380)

Key Mechanisms & Goals

  • Tech Tax → UBI: Economy funded by machine taxation; Universal Basic Income as technological dividend; humans tax-free

  • Post-scarcity sustainability: Machine productivity, renewable energy, and circular economies eliminate structural scarcity

  • Transparency & ecology: Open-source algorithms, blockchain verification, and ecological integration as foundational principles

  • Ultimate objectives: Elimination of war, longevity for all, planetary administration based on equality and human flourishing

Source: Encyclopedia.pub entry 59380 • Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18028339 • Goeritz R. (2024)

Core Definition: The Architecture of Electric Technocracy

A Synthesis of Human Will and Artificial Intelligence

Electric Technocracy is defined as a post-national governance architecture that fundamentally reconfigures the relationship between technology, labor, and law. Unlike historical technocratic movements, it does not cede sovereignty to a "rule of experts" or machines. Instead, it utilizes Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) as a non-sovereign advisory layer. The ASI provides the computational power necessary for planetary-scale simulations and real-time resource optimization, while the actual decision-making authority remains exclusively in the hands of the collective human population through Direct Digital Democracy (DDD).

The Economic Engine: Tech Tax and the Universal Dividend

The system's economic validity is predicated on the transition from human labor to machine productivity. In an Electric Technocracy, humans are freed from the tax burden, which is shifted entirely to a comprehensive Technology Tax levied on automated systems and robotic output. This "Technological Dividend" funds a global Universal Basic Income (UBI), effectively decoupling human survival from market participation. By treating machine labor as a common planetary asset, the system eliminates structural scarcity and poverty, allowing for a steady-state economy focused on sustainability and human flourishing.

Foundational Principles: Transparency and Longevity

The ultimate objectives of this model are the elimination of geopolitical conflict and the maximization of human potential. By replacing the fragmented nation-state system with a unified, blockchain-verified planetary administration, the drivers of war—territorial disputes and resource competition—are neutralized. The governance algorithms are entirely open-source, ensuring total transparency and public audibility. In this high-trust environment, collective resources are redirected toward longevity research and ecological restoration, securing a stable, thriving future for all digital citizens under the legal framework of the World Succession Deed 1400/98.
