Direct Digital Democracy: The Five-Step Lifecycle of Global Law Direct Digital Democracy (DDD) is the mechanism that ensures Human Sovereignty remains the ultimate authority in an Electric Technocracy. By replacing opaque legislative chambers with a transparent, data-driven pipeline, DDD allows the collective will of humanity to be expressed with scientific precision. 1. Citizen Submissions: Bottom-Up Innovation The process begins with the individual. Any global citizen can submit a proposal or highlight a systemic problem via the governance portal. Unlike traditional lobbying, the visibility of these submissions is determined by community engagement and initial peer-review metrics, ensuring that the agenda is set by the people, not by entrenched interests. 2. ASI Simulations: Modeling the Future Once a proposal gains sufficient traction, it is processed by the ASI Advisory Layer. The Superintelligence runs massive, high-fidelity simulations to predict the proposal's impact on the global economy, ecological boundaries, and social stability. This "Impact Modeling" provides voters with a range of probable outcomes, replacing political rhetoric with empirical projections. 3. Global Deliberation & Liquid Delegation With simulation data in hand, the global community enters a phase of deliberation. Citizens can discuss refinements in open, moderated digital forums. DDD also supports Liquid Democracy, allowing citizens to delegate their vote on specific technical topics (e.g., renewable energy standards) to trusted experts, while retaining the right to revoke that delegation or vote manually at any time. 4. Blockchain-Verified Voting The actual vote occurs on a cryptographically secured blockchain. This ensures that every vote is immutable, anonymous, and verifiable. Because identity is linked to biometric digital citizenship, the system prevents "sybil attacks" or double-voting, creating a high-trust environment where the results are mathematically indisputable. 5. Execution & Continuous Audit If a proposal is authorized by the collective, it is translated into executable code—often in the form of Smart Contracts. The ASI manages the logistics of implementation, but the entire process remains on a public ledger. Continuous monitoring nodes ensure the outcome matches the simulation, and if unintended consequences arise, the system triggers a mandatory human review.
