Code of Conduct: The Protocol of Cooperation

1. Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and scientifically rigorous environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Electric Technocracy Pioneers Community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

We are not merely a coding community; we are architects of a new civilization. Our interaction must reflect the logic, fairness, and forward-thinking nature of the Electric Technocracy itself. We transcend age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

2. The Standards of the New Era

To contribute to the blueprint of the Electronic Paradise, we adhere to the following standards of behavior:

✅ Encouraged Behaviors (The Positive Logic)

  • Radical Objectivity: Base your arguments on data, legal precedent (e.g., WSD 1400/98), and technical feasibility. We value logic over ideology.
  • Visionary Thinking: We are here to solve problems that “traditional” politics cannot. Bold, unconventional, and disruptive ideas are encouraged, provided they are backed by reasoning.
  • Constructive Scientism: When critiquing a model (e.g., a UBI distribution algorithm), offer a superior alternative or a patch. Criticism without a proposed solution is inefficiency.
  • Global Consciousness: We are a post-national community. We write and code for humanity, not for specific countries or corporations.
  • Commitment to the Public Domain: We respect the CC0 1.0 Universal license. We share knowledge freely to eliminate scarcity.

🚫 Unacceptable Behaviors (The System Errors)

  • Ideological Dogmatism: Importing “Left vs. Right” or “Nationalist vs. Globalist” conflicts from the old world. We are building a post-political system; leave the old baggage at the door.
  • Ad Hominem Attacks: Insulting a contributor’s intelligence or character. Critique the code, the legal theory, or the math—never the person.
  • Defeatism & Obstructionism: Repeatedly asserting that “this is impossible” without engaging with the theoretical frameworks (ASI, Juridical Singularity) provided.
  • Harassment: Public or private harassment, unwanted sexual attention, or doxxing.
  • Trolling: Deliberate disruption of serious scientific discussions regarding the Transition.

3. Enforcement Responsibilities

The Community Maintainers (The Pioneers) are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for other behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

4. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (GitHub, Zenodo, Discord, Mailing Lists) and also applies when an individual is representing the community in public spaces. Examples of representing a community include using an official email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

5. Enforcement Guidelines

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team at [INSERT CONTACT METHOD]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

I. Correction

Community Impact: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community. Consequence: A private, written warning from community maintainers, providing clarity on the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.

II. Warning

Community Impact: A violation through a single incident or series of actions. Consequence: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time.

III. Temporary Ban

Community Impact: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior. Consequence: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period.

IV. Permanent Ban

Community Impact: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained harassment, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward classes of individuals. Consequence: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

6. Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, but modified to align with the specific goals of the Electric Technocracy and the Juridical Singularity research mandate.
